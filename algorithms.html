Algorithm library

Every cipher and hash WinHash ships with, what it is good for, and whether you should trust it today. Use the filters to narrow the list.

Choosing safely: for real-world encryption use AES-256-GCM (or ChaCha20-Poly1305 where available) with a strong password. Classical ciphers are for learning; MD5 and SHA-1 are broken for security; CRC32 and FNV-1a are checksums, not protection.

Security levels at a glance

LevelMeaningExamples
StrongSafe for protecting real data today.AES-256-GCM, ChaCha20-Poly1305, RSA-OAEP-2048+, SHA-256, SHA3-256
ModerateUsable but with caveats — prefer stronger options.WinHash Hybrid, RIPEMD-160, RSA-1024
WeakEducational or legacy only — do not rely on it.Caesar, Vigenère, Rail Fence, XOR, SHA-1, MD5
ChecksumDetects accidental errors, provides no secrecy.CRC32, FNV-1a, Base64
